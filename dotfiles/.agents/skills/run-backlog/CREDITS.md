# Credits

The shape (a coordinator that never writes code, one fresh session per ticket, working the frontier of the blocking graph, never two writers on one area) follows Lauren Tan's (poteto) `autopilot-full` and `orchestrate` playbooks in [pstack](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/main/pstack) and her Oct 2026 livestream with Matt Pocock. No pstack text is reused: the dispatch runs through this harness's `lfg`, `pr-state.ts` and `implement` instead.
